# Clarity and flow audit of the qualification text (2026-11-16)

Scope: `quali/chapters/*.tex` and `quali/figures/*.tex`. This audit only reports problems; it does not edit any file. The reader in mind is an AI/ML researcher who does not specialise in IR or RAG. Line numbers refer to the source files. Each paragraph is one source line, so one line number points to a whole paragraph.

## (a) Introduction: diagnosis and proposed outline

### Diagnosis

1. **The line from problem to research questions has a gap.** Context (l.6, l.8) moves well from the cost of RAG to multi-hop questions. After that:
   - l.10 brings in three questions, including "isolated in its weights", with no reason given for why a reader should care about weights.
   - The cost consequence comes last, after those questions, so the paragraph reads backwards.
   - No paragraph says what is missing in existing work. That gap is stated only at the start of Chapter 3 (03:4).
   - RQ4 therefore comes from nowhere for this reader.
2. **Terms are used before they are explained.** These appear in the Introduction but are defined only in Chapters 2–4, or never:
   - selector, cross-encoder, forward pass, stopping rule, one-shot, iterative, conditioned query, chain-following, graft, router, adapter, confound.
   - The Introduction says "passage" while the rest of the text says "paragraph".
3. **The RQs, hypothesis, figure, contributions and results do not match.**
   - **RQ1** asks about savings "at equal evidence recall and answer quality". Chapter 5 (05:9) says equivalence was *not* established.
   - **RQ2** is answered only as "suggests" (05:37), and its pre-registered test was "not testable". There is no row for it in the claims table, yet contribution 2 states it as evidence.
   - **The hypothesis and Figure 1(b)** switch the adapter on for "long chains". Chapter 5 shows that a router based on hop count *failed*, and the router that works is trained on *benefit*. The figure therefore shows the refuted design.
   - **The router result** (exp14, confirmed) is missing from the contributions. It appears only as a half-sentence inside the hypothesis section.
   - **Contribution 3** says "about 85%". The abstract and claims table say 86.8% (exp12); 84.7% is the confounded exp11 number. The contribution also mixes two different findings: the rank-2 structure (RQ4) and the long-chain benefit (RQ3).
   - **RQ4** asks whether the change can be a "reusable component". Reuse (transfer, W5) has not been tested yet, and the text should say so.
   - **The paper title** says "Low-Rank Skill", but the Introduction never uses "skill".
4. **The hypothesis paragraph (l.25) is the hardest sentence in the chapter.** It mixes the claim, the design, partial evidence ("functionally", "grafting an extracted change") and the router in three sentences. The status of the evidence belongs in the contributions or in the summary of results.
5. **Organization (l.45)** lists the chapters but does not say which chapter answers which RQ.
